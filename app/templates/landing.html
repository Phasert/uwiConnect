How UWI Connect works

Three steps from signing in to staying informed.

    Sign in to your alumni account

    Use a verified UWI alumni account. Only eligible alumni can submit listings.

  2. 2

    Explore available jobs and events

    Browse opportunities shared for the alumni community.

  3. 3

    Share opportunities and stay informed

    Submit a listing or follow notifications. An administrator reviews every submission before publication.
